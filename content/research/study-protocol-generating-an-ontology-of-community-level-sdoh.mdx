This study protocol proposes the development of an  ontology is to organize SDOH concepts across community-level, place-based, regional, population-level, and policy-relevant determinants of health. The ontology will identify candidate terms across twelve primary SDOH domains, review their use in the literature and existing ontology research, refine their definitions and placement, and translate them into a formal ontology structure. It introduces a community-level and place-based semantic framework for understanding how SDOH terms are defined, classified, related, and positioned across domains, measurement scales, and policy contexts. More broadly, the ontology will make the conceptual boundaries, meanings, and levels of analysis associated with SDOH terms explicit, providing a foundation for more consistent measurement, study comparison, data interoperability, and future public health and policy applications.
