description: Many spatial analyses rely on a measure of distance.While
  straight-line distance (“as the crow flies”) is appropriate for some
  use-cases, we often want transportation measures that better reflect the
  barriers between people and places. Transportation has an outsized effect on
  our health because it shapes how our communities are designed, the
  opportunities available to us, and environmental exposures like heat and air
  quality
