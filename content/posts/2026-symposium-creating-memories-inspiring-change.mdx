Thank you to everyone who joined us in Chicago for an exchange of ideas, research, and approaches to understanding SDOH through a place-based lens.

This year's symposium brought together:

👥 69 registered attendees

🎤 33 speakers

📊 27 sessions

Across presentations, workshops, and showcases, participants explored innovative research, shared tools and resources, and discussed opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration to advance health equity.

Beyond the numbers, we're especially grateful for the connections made and perspectives shared throughout the event.
