## **Help Shape How Community Health Is Understood**

The SDOH & Place Project is seeking community members, community and public health leaders, researchers, and others with relevant lived or professional experience to participate in a research study focused on developing a **community-level Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) ontology**. \
\
Participants will help us understand whether the concepts, language, and relationships used to describe community conditions and structural influences on health accurately reflect real-world experiences. \
\
Adults aged 18 or older who can participate in English and are willing to participate in an audio-recorded virtual focus group are invited to learn more.

**Interested in participating? Complete our brief Interest & Eligibility Screening Form to learn more and determine whether the study may be a good fit for you.**

Complete the Screening Form & Sign Up Here: [go.illinois.edu/OntologyStudyParticipation](https://go.illinois.edu/OntologyStudyParticipation)
